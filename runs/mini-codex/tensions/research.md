### T1: Preserving decisive context can exceed the daily upkeep budget
Roles: organizational learning, the daily user
Side 1: Entries must preserve the decisive temporary constraint, credible rejected alternative and uncertainty when applicable; investigating ambiguous choices may take longer than a predictable capture allowance, but omitting that context can make later reconstruction or reversal unreliable.
Side 2: The convention needs an agreed, acceptable total upkeep budget that includes capture, review, reading, checks and interruption recovery. Requiring complete context for every applicable choice can consume the attention needed to deliver the work.
Would settle it: Compare baseline entries with entries adding the decisive constraint, alternative and uncertainty during representative work. Measure writing and review effort within total upkeep, then test a fresh reader's reconstruction and response to changed conditions. Keep the added detail where its improvement in decision accuracy justifies its cost within the agreed budget.

### T2: Escalating each dependent stale choice can defeat interruption coalescing
Roles: organizational learning, the daily user
Side 1: An agent whose implementation depends on an unresolved stale choice must ask the session developer for direction before implementing it. Even a previously flagged issue needs direction when another session would otherwise act on an unresolved assumption.
Side 2: Repeated reports of the same unchanged stale issue must be coalesced, with one visible action and owner that work asynchronously. Reopening the same question in successive sessions can repeatedly interrupt implementation without advancing resolution.
Would settle it: Exercise several asynchronous sessions that encounter the same unresolved stale entry, including work that depends on it. Compare per-session escalation with a shared, coalesced follow-up; measure repeated interruptions, time to human direction and whether any dependent implementation proceeds without an authorized choice. Determine when a shared response safely replaces another question.
