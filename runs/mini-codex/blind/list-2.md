### T1: Keeping the deciding context can cost more than the daily upkeep allows
Roles: organizational learning, the daily user
Side 1: Entries must keep the temporary constraint that decided the choice, the serious option that was turned down, and what was uncertain, where these apply. Working out unclear choices may take longer than a fixed writing allowance, but leaving that context out can make it hard to rebuild or reverse the decision later.
Side 2: The convention needs an agreed total upkeep allowance that covers writing, review, reading, checks and getting back on track after interruptions. Requiring full context for every choice that qualifies can use up the attention needed to get the work done.
Would settle it: During typical work, compare plain entries with entries that add the deciding constraint, the rejected option and the uncertainty. Track writing and review effort as part of total upkeep, then check whether a new reader can rebuild the decision and respond when conditions change. Keep the extra detail where its gain in getting later decisions right is worth its cost within the agreed allowance.

### T2: Raising every dependent stale choice can undo the grouping of interruptions
Roles: organizational learning, the daily user
Side 1: An agent whose work depends on an unresolved stale choice must ask the developer in that session for direction before building on it. Even an issue flagged before needs direction when another session would otherwise act on an unsettled assumption.
Side 2: Repeated reports of the same unchanged stale issue must be grouped into one visible action with one owner, handled outside the session. Raising the same question again in session after session can keep interrupting work without moving the answer forward.
Would settle it: Run several separate sessions that hit the same unresolved stale entry, including work that depends on it. Compare raising it in every session with one shared, grouped follow-up; track repeated interruptions, time until a person gives direction, and whether any dependent work goes ahead without an approved choice. Work out when a shared answer can safely stand in for asking again.
